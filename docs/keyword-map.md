## 4. Tier 3: Material Specification Sheets (GEO & Citable Data)

Every material page contains standardized physical property tables, chemical resistance metrics, and clear trade-off profiles:

### 4.1 PLA Pro Eco (`/materials/pla`)
- **Primary Keyword**: `pla 3d printing service india`
- **Tensile Strength**: 48–55 MPa
- **Heat Deflection Temp (HDT)**: 55°C
- **Key Use Cases**: Proof-of-concept mockups, architectural study models, indoor enclosures, consumer gift items.
- **When NOT to Choose**: High-temperature environments (e.g., automotive engine bays, direct summer dashboards) or prolonged outdoor moisture.

### 4.2 Industrial PETG (`/materials/petg`)
- **Primary Keyword**: `industrial petg 3d printing`
- **Tensile Strength**: 50–58 MPa
- **HDT**: 75°C
- **Key Use Cases**: Water-tight ducting, electrical junction boxes, food-contact containers, chemical lab splash guards.
- **When NOT to Choose**: High-friction mechanical gears without lubrication or continuous high-load cyclic fatigue.

### 4.3 ABS-ESD / Flame Retardant (`/materials/abs`)
- **Primary Keyword**: `abs 3d printing service india`
- **Tensile Strength**: 42–48 MPa
- **HDT**: 98°C
- **Key Use Cases**: Electronic hardware chassis, drone power distribution boxes, industrial sensor housings.
- **When NOT to Choose**: Delicate micro-miniatures requiring optical resin clarity; SLA is better.

### 4.4 Carbon-X PA12 (Carbon Fiber Reinforced Nylon) (`/materials/carbon-fiber`)
- **Primary Keyword**: `carbon fiber 3d printing service india`
- **Tensile Strength**: 85–110 MPa
- **Flexural Modulus**: 7,500 MPa
- **Key Use Cases**: Drone arms, racing suspension brackets, robotics end-of-arm tooling (EOAT), replacement metal brackets.
- **When NOT to Choose**: Decorative or non-structural ornamental pieces where standard PLA provides identical visual appearance at 1/3 the cost.

### 4.5 TPU 95A Elastomer (`/materials/tpu`)
- **Primary Keyword**: `flexible tpu 3d printing service`
- **Shore Hardness**: 95A
- **Elongation at Break**: >450%
- **Key Use Cases**: Vibration isolators, shock-absorbing drone bumper feet, custom gaskets, wearable ergonomics.
- **When NOT to Choose**: Rigid structural load-bearing housings or precision gear teeth.

### 4.6 Ultra-SLA Tough & Ceramic Resins (`/materials/resin`)
- **Primary Keyword**: `precision resin 3d printing service`
- **Resolution**: 25 to 50 microns
- **Surface Roughness**: Ra < 1.0 μm
- **Key Use Cases**: Micro-fluidic chips, jewelry masters, medical anatomical models, high-detail display scale models.
- **When NOT to Choose**: Parts subjected to severe impact drop tests onto concrete.
